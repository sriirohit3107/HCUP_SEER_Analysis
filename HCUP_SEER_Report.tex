\section{SEER Data}
% =============================================================================

\subsection*{Q1. Is it based on county or state level?}
\begin{answerbox}
The current SEER export does \textbf{not} include county or state-level geographic fields. SEER data is organized by \textbf{cancer registry regions} (e.g., SEER 9, SEER 17, SEER 21), which cover specific geographic areas but are not mapped to individual counties or states in our export.
\end{answerbox}

\vspace{6pt}

\subsection*{Q2. Could you look into the counts of the fall patients?}
\begin{answerbox}
\textbf{Not applicable for SEER.} SEER is a cancer registry and only captures cancer diagnoses, staging, histology, and survival outcomes. It does not contain fall-related events or injury diagnoses. Fall data is available in the \textbf{HCUP NIS} dataset (see Section~2).
\end{answerbox}

\vspace{6pt}

\subsection*{Q3. Number of Alzheimer's patients?}
\begin{answerbox}
\textbf{Not applicable for SEER.} SEER tracks cancer diagnoses only and does not capture Alzheimer's disease or other neurological conditions. Alzheimer's patient counts are available in the \textbf{HCUP NIS} dataset (see Section~2).
\end{answerbox}

\vspace{6pt}

\section{HCUP NIS Data (2016--2022)}
% =============================================================================

\noindent\textbf{Data Source:} Healthcare Cost and Utilization Project (HCUP) --- National Inpatient Sample (NIS)\\
\textbf{Coverage:} 2016--2022 \quad \textbf{Total years:} 7 \quad \textbf{Total discharges:} $\sim$48.2 million

\vspace{6pt}

\subsection*{Q1. Is it based on county or state level?}
\begin{answerbox}
\textbf{No --- HCUP NIS does not include patient county or state of residence.} This is by design to protect patient privacy. The available geographic variables are:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \texttt{HOSP\_DIVISION} --- Census Division of the hospital (9 divisions)
  \item \texttt{HOSP\_REGION} --- Census Region of the hospital (Northeast, Midwest, South, West)
  \item \texttt{PL\_NCHS} --- NCHS Urban--Rural classification of patient ZIP code
\end{itemize}
Analysis can be performed at the \textbf{regional} or \textbf{urban-rural} level, but not at county or state level.
\end{answerbox}

\vspace{6pt}

\subsection*{Q2. Counts of Fall Patients}
\begin{answerbox}
Fall patients were identified using \textbf{ICD-10-CM codes W00--W19} across all diagnosis fields (\texttt{I10\_DX1} through \texttt{I10\_DX40}). For 2016, external cause fields (\texttt{I10\_ECAUSE1--4}) were also included.
\end{answerbox}
